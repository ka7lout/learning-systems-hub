# IHLS Research Hypotheses and Measurement Plan

All items below are research hypotheses/design decisions, not established product results.

| Hypothesis | Comparison | Outcomes | Safeguards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learner-selected state adaptation may improve retention and/or sustainability versus a fixed protocol. | Adaptive task size/scaffolding vs fixed lesson presentation, with content and total practice opportunity controlled. | Immediate, delayed and transfer performance; initiation friction selected voluntarily; attrition. | No diagnosis; accessible alternatives; consent; no sensitive clinical profiling. |
| Attempt-first AI assistance may improve independent performance compared with answer-first assistance. | Guided attempt/hint flow vs full answer first, on matched tasks. | Independent delayed solve, transfer, calibrated confidence, help dependency. | Allow explicit reference explanation; do not grade private student data without consent. |
| Tiny-task scaffolding may lower task initiation friction without reducing transfer. | Small decomposed tasks vs conventional task prompt. | Start completion, completion quality, delayed transfer, self-reported friction. | Avoid equating speed with ability. |
| Retrieval plus transfer plus a decision case may better support transfer than retrieval alone. | Retrieval-only vs retrieval+novel transfer/case. | Delayed retention and novel problem performance. | Predefine rubric; blind scoring where feasible. |
| Contextual vocabulary practice may improve technical English usage. | Contextual writing/speaking tasks vs isolated vocabulary exposure. | Reading, writing, speaking and interaction rubric scores. | Do not infer CEFR level from word count; validate speech tools before metrics. |
| Externalizing next steps and distraction capture may reduce avoidable context switching. | Optional task capture and next-step externalization vs no prompt. | Self-reported friction, session continuity, quality and sustainability. | No invasive tracking; no mental-health claim. |

## Scientific boundaries

Pre-register where feasible, document assignment, comparison groups, recruitment, attrition and analysis, run delayed tests, report effect uncertainty and limitations, assess subgroup safety and invite replication. An observational product dashboard is not a causal experiment. Never claim “best,” “proven,” clinical efficacy or Nobel relevance based on product analytics.
