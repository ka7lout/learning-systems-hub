# Career Engine

## Target-role inputs

The initial Navisoft-style and Nuwave-style skill lists are learner-supplied blueprints. They are not verified live job postings, not universal hiring criteria, and not an employment guarantee. The dossier records that no current first-party posting was verified during the research pass.

## Gap logic

Role requirement -> normalized skill -> curriculum node -> independent evidence -> artifact -> gap -> next task. “No evidence yet” is distinct from “skill absent.” Resume bullets require an inspectable repository/commit, deployment, report, metric source or other real artifact. Generated claims cannot invent impact, users, clients or results.

## Readiness

Use role-specific evidence gates and states (developing, competent, independently demonstrated, evidence missing). Do not collapse them into a misleading percentage or hiring prediction. Separate preferred from hard requirements only after a dated job snapshot verifies the source.

## Market snapshots

A future snapshot stores source URL, date, region, seniority, role family, requirements, confidence and current/historical status. Prefer lawful official career pages or permitted feeds; do not scrape behind controls or violate terms. Historical snapshots are append-only.

## Freelancing

Client discovery, requirements, scope, acceptance criteria, estimates, proposal, negotiation, change control, delivery and support can be practiced in clearly labeled simulations. Simulated clients never appear as real clients, work or revenue.
