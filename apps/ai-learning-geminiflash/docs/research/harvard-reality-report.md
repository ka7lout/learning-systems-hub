## 1. Executive Findings: Harvard Reality

### A. Harvard College Undergraduate Degree in "AI Engineering"
- **Status:** `not_found`
- **Official Finding:** Harvard College confers the Bachelor of Arts (A.B.) or Bachelor of Science (S.B.) in **Computer Science** or **Engineering Sciences (Electrical and Computer Engineering track)** within the John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS). There is NO standalone undergraduate degree titled "Artificial Intelligence Engineering".
- **Integration Action:** We preserve all original curriculum modules and layer Harvard CS/ECE requirements over them without making false claims about Harvard's degree taxonomy.

### B. Harvard College Computer Science Core Requirements
- **Status:** `confirmed_current`
- **Key Courses & Sequences:**
  1. **CS50**: Introduction to Computer Science (C, memory, pointers, data structures, Python, SQL, web).
  2. **CS51**: Abstraction and Design in Computation (functional programming, OCaml, abstraction barriers, formal reasoning).
  3. **CS61**: Systems Programming and Machine Organization (C, assembly, memory hierarchy, caching, concurrency, virtual memory).
  4. **CS1200 / CS1210 / CS1240**: Formal Languages, Theory of Computation, Data Structures and Algorithms.
  5. **CS1810 / CS181**: Machine Learning (probabilistic modeling, kernel methods, deep learning, reinforcement learning).
  6. **CS1820**: Artificial Intelligence (search, constraint satisfaction, Markov decision processes, games).
  7. **CS1650 / CS165**: Data Systems (column-stores, index structures, query optimization, storage engines).

### C. Harvard Mathematics & Statistics Foundations
- **Status:** `confirmed_current`
- **Key Courses:**
  1. **MATH 21A/B or 22A/B**: Multivariable Calculus & Linear Algebra.
  2. **STAT 110**: Introduction to Probability (Joe Blitzstein, conditioning, Markov chains, random variables, distribution zoo).
  3. **STAT 111**: Introduction to Statistical Inference (maximum likelihood, hypothesis testing, Bayesian inference).
  4. **APMTH 120 / CS1280**: Applied Mathematics & Convex Optimization for Machine Learning.

### D. Harvard Extension School Graduate Offerings
- **Status:** `confirmed_current`
- **Key Programs:**
  1. **Graduate Certificate in Artificial Intelligence**: 4 graduate-level courses covering Foundations of AI, Advanced NLP, Deep Learning, and AI Ethics/Governance.
  2. **Master of Liberal Arts (ALM) in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence**: Graduate degree incorporating Data Engineering, CSCI E-89 Deep Learning, CSCI E-222 Foundations of Large Language Models, CSCI E-104 Advanced Deep Learning, and APCOMP 215 / CSCI E-29 Scalable AI Systems.
