# IHLS research dossier · April 2026

## Verification method

Primary official pages were prioritized. A source is encoded as `confirmed_current` only when the current page supports the claim. Public snippets do not justify invented weekly schedules, grading weights, or hidden assignments.

## Harvard College CS

**Source:** Harvard Computer Science Advising, [Concentration Requirements](https://csadvising.seas.harvard.edu/concentration/requirements/), accessed April 2026. **Status:** confirmed_current.

The current page describes a basic plan with nine core CS courses across programming, formal reasoning, systems, computation and the world, and advanced computer science, plus 2–5 mathematics courses including preparation, linear algebra, and probability. This is the basis for the added CS foundations and math nodes. It does not prove a standalone undergraduate degree titled “AI Engineering.”

**Source:** [CS50 College Fall 2026 syllabus](https://cs50.harvard.edu/college/2026/fall/syllabus/), accessed April 2026. **Status:** confirmed_current, term-specific.

The syllabus explicitly lists lectures, eleven sections, ten problem sets, three quizzes, and a final project. It also gives a term-specific AI policy. IHLS borrows the visible learning loop but does not claim to reproduce Harvard delivery or policy.

## Harvard Extension

**Source:** [Artificial Intelligence Graduate Certificate](https://extension.harvard.edu/academics/programs/artificial-intelligence-graduate-certificate/), accessed April 2026. **Status:** confirmed_current.

The official page describes four online graduate courses spanning AI foundations, advanced NLP/ML, deep learning/computer vision, and AI ethics/governance/law. It is an Extension offering and is not merged with Harvard College requirements.

**Source:** [Data Science Graduate Certificate](https://extension.harvard.edu/academics/programs/data-science-graduate-certificate/), accessed April 2026. **Status:** confirmed_current.

The page describes statistics, electives, a data science course, Python, wrangling, machine learning, and reproducible workflows. Course choices vary by term, so the product records the program layer without inventing a fixed course list.

**Source:** [Data Science and Artificial Intelligence Master’s Degree Requirements](https://extension.harvard.edu/academics/programs/data-science-graduate-program/data-science-degree-requirements/), accessed April 2026. **Status:** confirmed_current.

The official requirements describe foundations, statistical data modeling, data science core/electives, precapstone, and capstone. It is a Harvard Extension master’s degree path with its own requirements, not Harvard College undergraduate CS.

## Learning science

**Source:** Carpenter, Pan, and Butler, [The science of effective learning with spacing and retrieval practice](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-022-00089-1), Nature Reviews Psychology, 2022. **Status:** confirmed_current as a peer-reviewed review.

The product uses retrieval and spacing as design inputs with context-aware scheduling, not as a claim that one fixed interval is universally optimal. Transfer checks and independent evidence are design hypotheses that require future evaluation.

## Security and accessibility

**Sources:** [OWASP API Security Top 10 2023](https://owasp.org/API-Security/editions/2023/en/0x11-t10/) and [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). **Status:** confirmed_current standards/guidance.

The build therefore avoids client-supplied ownership IDs, arbitrary code execution, unsupported AI success claims, and inaccessible focus/motion defaults. Full auth, rate limiting, audit logging, file scanning, and multi-tenant regression tests remain production gates.

## Research hypotheses retained

- State-adaptive task size may reduce initiation friction.
- Retrieval plus transfer may improve novel-problem performance beyond recall-only practice.
- Requiring an initial attempt may preserve independent performance compared with answer-first tutoring.
- Contextual technical vocabulary practice may improve professional usage.

These are not outcomes. They need preregistered comparisons, delayed retention, transfer, subgroup analysis, ethics review where appropriate, and replication.
